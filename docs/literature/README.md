# Literature Support

This directory stores external literature-support artefacts used for planning
and manuscript development.

- [deep_research_hydrological_reaction_norms_2026-05-31.md](deep_research_hydrological_reaction_norms_2026-05-31.md):
  broad ChatGPT Deep Research review of hydrological reaction norms for
  Australia's nomadic waterbirds.
- [deep_research_hydrological_reaction_norms_triage_2026-05-31.md](deep_research_hydrological_reaction_norms_triage_2026-05-31.md):
  supervisor triage note describing how the review should and should not be
  used.
- [source_verification_queue_2026-05-31.md](source_verification_queue_2026-05-31.md):
  priority queue for directed source verification before manuscript claims are
  made from the review.
- [reports/002-traceable-literature-mining.md](reports/002-traceable-literature-mining.md):
  GPT-5.5 Pro second-pass source-mining report with annotated sources,
  verification caveats, and a claim-to-citation matrix.
- [identifiers/nomadic_waterbirds_identifiers.txt](identifiers/nomadic_waterbirds_identifiers.txt):
  Zotero-ready identifier list extracted from the second-pass report.
- [triage/002-supervisor-triage.md](triage/002-supervisor-triage.md):
  supervisor triage of the second-pass report and its manuscript implications.
- [tables/literature_claim_matrix.csv](tables/literature_claim_matrix.csv):
  machine-readable claim-to-citation matrix from the second-pass report.
- [verification/manual_verification_queue.csv](verification/manual_verification_queue.csv):
  machine-readable queue of sources and documentation streams requiring manual
  verification before manuscript use.

## Use Policy

Broad Deep Research reports are orientation and planning artefacts. They can
shape goals, modelling design, conceptual figures, and literature-search
priorities. They are not claim-grade evidence until individual sources and
citation details have been verified.

Traceable literature-mining reports are stronger than broad overviews because
they map sources to claims and provide stable identifiers. They still require
source-level reading before claim-grade manuscript use.
